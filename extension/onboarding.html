Welcome to Coursepilot

The fastest way through online homework.

One extension. Two platforms. Endless homework done.

Coursepilot supports both McGraw Hill Recharge and Pearson MyLab. On Recharge, it reads McGraw's own answer data — 100% accuracy, zero AI cost, instant. On MyLab, it uses your OpenAI key to power a fast solver across every question type you'll encounter.

McGraw Hill

Recharge

100% accuracy. No API key required. Multiple choice, fill-in-the-blank, true/false, ordering, matching, multi-select.

Pearson

MyLab Review

~95% accuracy via your OpenAI key. Multiple choice, multi-select, sorting, ranking, vocab fill-in. BYOK.

1
Activate your license
Click the CP icon in your Chrome toolbar. Paste your license key and hit Activate. You'll see a green dot when it's accepted.
2
(Pearson only) Add your OpenAI key
Open settings (gear icon) and paste an OpenAI API key starting with sk-. Recharge users can skip this step.
3
Open an assignment and click Start
Go to learning.mheducation.com or your Pearson MyLab page. Open the Coursepilot popup and hit the big Start button. The bot answers each question, picks confidence, and advances. Watch your accuracy climb live.
